About

Made for people who post a lot and don't want to write it four times

PostCraft AI helps turn one piece of content into platform-specific social posts, saving time while keeping each version natural and relevant to its audience.

A LinkedIn post should read like a LinkedIn post. A tweet should not be a paragraph. PostCraft handles the reshaping so you can stay focused on the idea, not the formatting.
